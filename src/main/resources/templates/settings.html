Auto-forward from Gmail

  1. Gmail → Settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → add the address above.
  2. Create a filter, e.g. from:(pge.com OR coned.com OR xfinity.com).
  3. Choose Forward it to your Bill Tracker address.
